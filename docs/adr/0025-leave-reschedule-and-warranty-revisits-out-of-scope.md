# ADR-0025: Leave reschedule and warranty revisits out of scope

- Status: Accepted (default — owner may revise)
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Source: PRD §13; docs/build/04-decisions.md D16

## Context

The PRD leaves this question open (§13). The build needs a default to stay autonomous (D16).

## Decision

Rescheduling and warranty revisits wait until the owner adds rules to the PRD (Figma section 13 proposals).

## Consequences

The owner may revise this default; changing it later means a new ADR that supersedes this one, not a silent edit.
