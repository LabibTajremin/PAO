# ADR-0013: Accept cash payments only

- Status: Accepted (default — owner may revise)
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Source: PRD §13; docs/build/04-decisions.md D4

## Context

The PRD leaves this question open (§13). The build needs a default to stay autonomous (D4).

## Decision

Payment is cash. The provider confirms "cash received" at completion. Digital payment is Phase 2 (C-17).

## Consequences

The owner may revise this default; changing it later means a new ADR that supersedes this one, not a silent edit.
