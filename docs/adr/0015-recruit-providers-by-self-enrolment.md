# ADR-0015: Recruit providers by self-enrolment

- Status: Accepted (default — owner may revise)
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Source: PRD §13; docs/build/04-decisions.md D6

## Context

The PRD leaves this question open (§13). The build needs a default to stay autonomous (D6).

## Decision

Providers enrol themselves through the Partner app.

## Consequences

The owner may revise this default; changing it later means a new ADR that supersedes this one, not a silent edit.
