# ADR-0011: Launch with five MVP services

- Status: Accepted (default — owner may revise)
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Source: PRD §13; docs/build/04-decisions.md D2

## Context

The PRD leaves this question open (§13). The build needs a default to stay autonomous (D2).

## Decision

Electrician, Plumber, AC service and Home salon (on-demand jobs) plus Driver (1-day duration hire only). Seeded from `backend/seed/catalog.yaml`.

## Consequences

The owner may revise this default; changing it later means a new ADR that supersedes this one, not a silent edit.
