# ADR-0010: Brand the product PAO and the provider app PAO Partner

- Status: Accepted (default — owner may revise)
- Date: 2026-10-09
- Source: PRD §13; docs/build/04-decisions.md D1

## Context

The PRD leaves this question open (§13). The build needs a default to stay autonomous (D1).

## Decision

Public brand **PAO**; partner app **PAO Partner**. The codename `lift` is not used in code, package names or identifiers.

## Consequences

The owner may revise this default; changing it later means a new ADR that supersedes this one, not a silent edit.
